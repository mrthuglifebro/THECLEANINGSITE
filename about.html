About The Cleaning Verdict

Built by someone who actually cares about cleaning.

Why this site exists

I've always been genuinely passionate about cleaning, the kind of passion most people don't understand until their kitchen is spotless at midnight. After years of testing products, wasting money on gimmicks, and discovering a handful of things that actually work, I built The Cleaning Verdict so nobody else has to go through the same trial and error.

What makes this different

Most cleaning content online is either sponsored, vague, or written by someone who's never actually dealt with the mess they're talking about. The Cleaning Verdict is different: every product here is categorized honestly, Verdict Scores come from real user reviews (not made-up numbers), and our compare feature tells you which product actually wins, not just puts two boxes side by side.

How it works

Search by product name or brand, filter by the specific mess you're dealing with, compare products side by side, and read real reviews from people who've actually used them. If you think a product deserves to be added, you can suggest it directly and we'll take a look.

Follow along

We also post cleaning content, product reactions, and honest reviews on social media. Come find us and say hi.
